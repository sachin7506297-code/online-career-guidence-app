💡 About the Project

CareerPath for Every Student

A student-focused portal designed to make career information easier to discover.

What is this portal?

CareerPath is a frontend project concept for an Online Career Guidance Portal for Underprivileged Students. It combines career exploration, a simple self-assessment, scholarship discovery, free learning resources and official career links.

Why was it created?

Students can find it difficult to compare career choices, understand education pathways or discover financial support. This portal organizes information into simple sections so students can explore options and make more informed decisions.

Objectives

  • Make career information easy to understand.
  • Help students identify career areas matching their interests and skills.
  • Connect students with learning and scholarship resources.
  • Encourage verification through official sources.
  • Provide a responsive and accessible student experience.

Mission

To make reliable career information and learning opportunities more accessible to students from every financial background.

Future Scope

A future version can add a secure backend, counselor accounts, real scholarship APIs, student progress tracking, multilingual content and verified databases.
